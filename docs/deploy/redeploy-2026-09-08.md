# Redeploy on chain 5042002, 2026-09-08

The settlement stack was redeployed from `0xaFF9CD31ae93e1bdD70FFDf0763C2e010037c65c`
with `contracts/script/deploy-arc-testnet.sh`, after the four findings in
[#89](https://github.com/wienerlabs/square/issues/89),
[#90](https://github.com/wienerlabs/square/issues/90),
[#91](https://github.com/wienerlabs/square/issues/91) and
[#92](https://github.com/wienerlabs/square/issues/92) changed storage layouts
and constructor signatures. The previous addresses (SquareJob
`0x2570a151…`, KeeperEvaluator `0x6c62D57B…`, Arbitration `0xA10C2e9f…`,
ClaimMarket `0xc5495bc5…`, SquareHook `0x92EC31aA…`) stay on chain with
their history and are no longer referenced by the SDK, the app or the docs.
The decision behind the expiry rule is in
[docs/decisions/expiry-after-submission.md](../decisions/expiry-after-submission.md).

## What changed on chain

| Finding | Change |
|---|---|
| #89 receivable routing | `ClaimMarket.list` refuses a job whose hook does not resolve the payout to this market (`IPayoutResolver.payoutMarket()`), and `payeeOf` reads `SquareJob.providerOf` |
| #90 expiry after submission | `claimRefund` refuses a `Submitted` job under an evaluator with a settlement horizon (`SettledByEvaluator`); `settlementHorizon()` gained `finalizeGrace` |
| #91 locked bond | `Arbitration.settleBond` is permissionless and returns the bond to the disputer when the job is `Expired` or `Rejected` |
| #92 description gas | `createJob` caps the description at `MAX_DESCRIPTION = 256` bytes (`DescriptionTooLong`) |
