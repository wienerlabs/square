# Redeploy on chain 5042002, 2026-09-09

The settlement stack was redeployed from `0xaFF9CD31ae93e1bdD70FFDf0763C2e010037c65c`
with `contracts/script/deploy-arc-testnet.sh` after the second review round
changed constructor signatures, the job record and the market's `buy`
signature ([#100](https://github.com/wienerlabs/square/issues/100),
[#101](https://github.com/wienerlabs/square/issues/101),
[#110](https://github.com/wienerlabs/square/issues/110),
[#111](https://github.com/wienerlabs/square/issues/111),
[#112](https://github.com/wienerlabs/square/issues/112),
[#113](https://github.com/wienerlabs/square/issues/113),
[#114](https://github.com/wienerlabs/square/issues/114),
[#117](https://github.com/wienerlabs/square/issues/117),
[#135](https://github.com/wienerlabs/square/issues/135)). The decisions behind
the behaviour changes are in
[hook-failure-modes.md](../decisions/hook-failure-modes.md) and
[lapsed-bond.md](../decisions/lapsed-bond.md). The 2026-09-08 addresses
(SquareJob `0x32E64208…`, KeeperEvaluator `0xD9f9137f…`, Arbitration
`0x0Ad6268d…`, ClaimMarket `0x32eD0Ef1…`, SquareHook `0xE61f8698…`) stay on
chain with their history and hold no balance any more (see below).

## What changed on chain

| Finding | Change |
|---|---|
| #100 hook lock | `complete` and `reject` tolerate a hook failure (`HookFailed`); `claimRefund` reopens after expiry when the resolver cannot answer (`PayoutUnresolvable`); the hook wraps its compliance module (`ComplianceCheckFailed`) |
| #101 grace, bond, probe | the horizon is pinned in the job record; `finalizeGrace` lives in the versioned `Window`; `settleBond`'s `Rejected` leg is gone; the market probes `payoutMarket()` under a gas cap |
| #110 hookless split | `Arbitration.vote` and `finalizeDecided` refuse a split without a payout resolver (`SplitNeedsAPayoutResolver`) |
| #111 roles and reputation | `ProviderIsEvaluator`; positive feedback only under the trusted evaluator and above `minReputationBudget` (`ReputationSkipped`) |
| #112 price binding | `ClaimMarket.buy(jobId, expectedPrice)` with `PriceMismatch` |
| #113 validation binding | the hook checks the bound request names it and the agent (`ValidationRequestMismatch`) |
| #135 window read | `challengeEndsAt` is zero for a job the keeper does not evaluate |
