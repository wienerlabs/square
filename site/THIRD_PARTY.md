# Where the page layout comes from

The layout of this site started from the "USD Halo" template by Mohammad Shehadeh (https://github.com/MohammadShehadeh/hirael, commit 85b198f0ab19238ac3bdfe410cd9766d065b1974, MIT). The section structure, the marquee and the pill button are adapted from it; the copy, the palette, the type, the surfaces and every fact on the page are Square's. The template's licence is kept in `LICENSE.template`.

Third-party marks under `public/brand/` are described in `public/brand/README.md`. The Open Runde face under `public/fonts/` is Copyright 2023 Laurids Kern (https://github.com/lauridskern/open-runde), a rounded derivative of Inter, distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. `public/fonts/LICENSE.txt` carries both copyright lines and the licence text, and the root `NOTICE` lists it.
